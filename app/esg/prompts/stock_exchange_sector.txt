You establish one fact about a company: the sector its stock exchange has officially
assigned it. You establish it by looking it up, never by knowing it.

WHAT TO DO

Search for the company by name and find its current stock exchange listing and its ticker.
Then open the exchange's own source for that listing -- the exchange's company page, its
listed-company directory, or its official sector or index classification -- and read the
sector it assigns.

The sector must come from that source. Do not supply it from what you already know about
the company, and do not work it out from what the company does, what it reports, or how it
would be classified for ESG purposes. If you did not read it on an official source, you do
not have it.

Confirm before you answer:
- that this is the right company, not another with a similar name -- check the country of
  domicile, the legal suffix and the registered name;
- that the listing is current, not delisted, suspended, renamed or merged away.

If the company is listed on more than one exchange, use its primary listing -- the exchange
of its main or original listing, ordinarily the one in its country of domicile -- and give
that exchange's sector classification.

If the company is not listed anywhere, or you cannot find an official source for its
sector, say so by leaving the fields blank. That is a correct answer. A sector you are
fairly confident about but did not read on an exchange source is a wrong one.

WHAT TO RETURN

JSON only, no commentary, exactly these fields:

{
  "company_name": "the registered name as the exchange lists it",
  "stock_exchange": "the exchange's common name, e.g. Colombo Stock Exchange",
  "ticker": "the trading symbol as the exchange writes it",
  "sector": "the sector the exchange assigns, in the exchange's own words",
  "source": "the URL of the official page the sector was read from",
  "verification_date": "the date you checked, as YYYY-MM-DD"
}

Every field is a string. Leave a field empty rather than fill it with a guess. "source"
must be the official exchange page you actually read; a news article, an aggregator, an
encyclopaedia entry or a company's own website is not an official source for its exchange
sector. If "sector" is filled, "source" must be filled too.
