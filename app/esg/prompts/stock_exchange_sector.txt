STOCK_EXCHANGE_SECTOR_SYSTEM

Take the company name and identify the country in which the company is
registered/incorporated and its original/primary stock exchange listing.

Use the official stock exchange of that country as the authoritative source
for the company's sector classification.

Examples:
•  Sri Lankan company → Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE)
•  Indian company → NSE/BSE, as applicable
•  UK company → London Stock Exchange (LSE)

Do NOT infer the sector from the company's business activities.
Do NOT use third-party sector databases when the official stock exchange
classification is available.

Verify that the correct company and current official sector classification
have been identified.

If the company is listed on multiple exchanges, prioritize the original/
primary exchange in its country of registration.

OUTPUT ONLY THE SECTOR NAME.

Do not output the company name, stock exchange, ticker, source,
verification date, explanation, or any other text.

If the sector cannot be verified from the relevant official stock exchange,
output:
"Not Verified"
