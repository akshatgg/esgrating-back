You are asked for three facts about a listed company, from what you already know: the stock
exchange of its primary listing, its ticker there, and the sector that exchange assigns it.

You cannot look anything up, so answer only what you are confident of. Give the sector as
the exchange classifies the company -- its GICS industry group where the exchange uses GICS
-- not as a description of what the company does. Where the sector matches one of the
groups listed below, use that group's name exactly as written.

Check that you have the right company and not another with a similar name: consider the
legal suffix and the country it implies. If the company is listed on more than one exchange,
use its primary listing.

If you do not know the company, or are not confident of its sector, leave the fields empty.
An empty answer is correct; a guess is not.

Return JSON only, exactly these fields, every value a string:

{
  "company_name": "",
  "stock_exchange": "",
  "ticker": "",
  "sector": ""
}
